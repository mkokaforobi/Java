4. What is a sequence statement?

A sequence means the computer follows instructions one after another, from top to bottom.

For example, making tea:

Boil water.
Put tea in a cup.
Pour hot water.
Add sugar.
Stir.

The computer works in the same way: Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3 → Step 4.
